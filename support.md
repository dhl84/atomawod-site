# AtomaWOD — Support


## Contact

To reach us, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/dhl84/atomawod-site/issues). Expect a reply within a few working days.

## Common questions

**The Readiness card does not appear.**
It needs Apple Health access and last night's data. Open Profile and tap
Connect Apple Health. If the row already says Connected, tap it once to grant
the newer metrics — the app asks for more data types than earlier versions did.

**A workout has no heart rate.**
Heart rate only exists if you wore an Apple Watch and started a workout on it.
Open the logged result and tap the effort line to attach a Watch workout by
hand. Profile → Sync past workouts fills in older sessions.

**I logged a score on the wrong day.**
Open the result and edit it. The date is editable on the log screen.

**My score did not save.**
The app queues the scores you log without a signal, then sends them when you
reconnect. The pending count appears in the app.

**I forgot my password.**
Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. The app emails you a code; there is
no link to click.
