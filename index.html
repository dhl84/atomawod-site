AtomaWOD

A training log built around per-movement history — for one athlete and their trainer.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026.

AtomaWOD needs an account, and it stores your training records on a server so your trainer can read them. This page says exactly what it stores, and what never leaves your phone.

What the app stores on its servers

The app uses Supabase for its database and sign-in. Supabase holds the data in the eu-west-1 region.

DataWhy
Your email address and account IDTo sign you in and to attach your records to you
Your name, role, and gymTo show your programming, and to separate trainer from athlete
Workouts, scores, per-movement results, RPE, and notesThe core purpose of the app
Heart rate summary for a workout you logged: average, maximum, duration, energyTo show effort for that session
A readiness score, a whole number from 0 to 100To show how ready you were on the day
App open times and the platform nameTo confirm the app is in use

What stays on your phone

If you connect Apple Health, the app reads these on your device and does not send them anywhere: sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, VO2 max, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, heart rate recovery, body weight, and lean body mass.

The app uses these values to draw the readiness card and the body composition card while you look at them. It does not write them to the database. It saves only the readiness score itself, a single number.

The app reads your Apple Watch workouts and their heart rate so it can attach the right workout to the session you logged. It saves the summary of that one workout. It does not save the raw heart rate samples.

What the app never does

  • It does not use your health or fitness data for advertising.
  • It does not sell your data.
  • It does not share your data with data brokers.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It contains no advertising code and no third-party analytics code.

Who else can see your data

  • Your trainer. A trainer at your gym can read the training records of athletes at that gym. That is the purpose of the app.
  • Supabase, as the hosting provider, processes the data on the owner's behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

No one else.

How long data is kept

The app keeps training records until you ask for them to be deleted. App open records have no automatic clean-up today.

Your choices

  • Withdraw Apple Health access at any time: iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → AtomaWOD. The app keeps working without it.
  • Export your data from the Profile tab.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask for it to be deleted, by email. Account deletion removes your training records and your app open records.

Children

AtomaWOD is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly hold their data.

Common questions

The Readiness card does not appear. It needs Apple Health access and last night's data. Open Profile and tap Connect Apple Health. If the row already says Connected, tap it once to grant the newer metrics — the app asks for more data types than earlier versions did.

A workout has no heart rate. Heart rate only exists if you wore an Apple Watch and started a workout on it. Open the logged result and tap the effort line to attach a Watch workout by hand. Profile → Sync past workouts fills in older sessions.

I logged a score on the wrong day. Open the result and edit it. The date is editable on the log screen.

My score did not save. The app queues the scores you log without a signal, then sends them when you reconnect. The pending count and a Retry uploads now control are on the History tab.

I forgot my password. Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. The app emails you a code; there is no link to click.

AtomaWOD does not give medical advice and is not a medical device.
